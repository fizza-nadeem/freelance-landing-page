# Freelancing Marketplace Platform

Hi! I'm Fizza Nadeem, and this is my 8-week internship project. I'm building a full freelancing marketplace platform from scratch.

What I'm Building

A platform where freelancers and clients can connect. Think of it like Fiverr or Upwork - but built by me!

Week 1: Landing Page Foundation

This week I built the foundation of the platform. Here's what I completed:

### What I Added:
- A professional navigation bar with logo and all links
- A hero section with a search bar (you can search for services or freelancers)
- Popular categories section showing 6 different service types
- A "How It Works" section explaining the platform
- Freelancer preview cards
- About section
- Footer with social media links

### What Makes It Special:
- The page works on phones, tablets, and computers
- The navigation collapses into a hamburger menu on mobile
- Clean design with blue as the primary color
- All links scroll smoothly to the right sections
